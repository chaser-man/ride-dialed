Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Information We Collect

Strava Integration

When you connect your Strava account to Dialed, we access:

  • Your cycling activities and their associated data (distance, time, power, heart rate)
  • Basic profile information (name, profile picture)
  • Activity segments and performance metrics

This data is retrieved through Strava's official API and is used solely to provide power analysis, training insights, and performance tracking within the app.

App Usage Data

We collect minimal usage data to improve the app experience:

  • App crashes and error reports
  • Feature usage statistics (anonymized)
  • Device type and iOS version (for compatibility)

Maintenance Data

Bike and component maintenance information you enter is stored locally on your device and synced across your Apple devices via iCloud when enabled.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide Dialed's core functionality:

  • Power Analysis: Calculate and display personal records, power curves, and performance benchmarks
  • Training Insights: Generate training load graphs and weekly performance summaries
  • Maintenance Tracking: Remind you of upcoming bike maintenance based on your riding patterns
  • App Improvement: Identify and fix bugs, optimize performance

We never use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose beyond improving your cycling experience.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

Most of your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure Core Data framework. This includes:

  • Cached Strava activities and analysis results
  • Bike and maintenance information
  • App preferences and settings

iCloud Sync

When enabled, your maintenance data syncs across your Apple devices using Apple's encrypted iCloud service. This data remains under your control and follows Apple's privacy standards.

Strava Token Security

Your Strava authentication tokens are stored securely in the iOS Keychain and are never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

Data Sharing

Dialed does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only data sharing that occurs is:

  • Strava API: We communicate with Strava's servers to fetch your activity data using your explicit authorization
  • Apple Services: Anonymous crash reports and usage analytics through Apple's standard frameworks

No personally identifiable information is shared in crash reports or analytics data.

Your Privacy Rights

Data Access and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Disconnect Strava: Revoke access at any time through the app settings
  • Data Export: Your data remains accessible in standard formats
  • Data Deletion: Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • iCloud Data: Manage through iOS Settings > Apple ID > iCloud

Strava Data Rights

Your Strava data remains under Strava's privacy policy. You can manage your Strava data permissions and revoke Dialed's access through your Strava account settings.

Children's Privacy

Dialed is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us to have it removed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do:

  • We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Significant changes will be communicated through the app
  • Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

Email: privacy@dialed.app

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Strava account?

+

During the welcome sequence, you'll be prompted to connect to Strava. Simply tap "Connect with Strava" and follow the authentication process. Your activities will automatically sync and remain connected.

Can I customize maintenance intervals?

+

Yes! Go to Settings > Maintenance Intervals to customize intervals for each bike component. You can set intervals based on distance, riding time, or total time.

What power metrics does Dialed analyze?

+

Dialed analyzes max power, average power, weighted average power, and total work (kJ). You can search for best power across any time interval and compare against recreational, amateur, or pro benchmarks.

How accurate is the training load graph?

+

The training load graph shows your actual weekly training data from Strava with light smoothing applied to reduce visual noise. The underlying data remains accurate while providing clearer trend visualization.

Does Dialed work offline?

+

Yes! Once your Strava data is synced, you can view all your maintenance schedules, power analysis, and training history offline. New activity syncing requires an internet connection.

Can I use metric or imperial units?

+

Absolutely! Go to Settings > Units to switch between metric (km, kg) and imperial (miles, lbs) measurements. All data will be converted automatically.
